A LITTLE ROOM FOR PLAY

Play, draw & color

No accounts, ads or internet needed. Share a screen, pick a game, or bring a coloring page to life.

Color a PDF or draw a picture

Open a coloring book from the library or a PDF on your device. Choose a page and paint with a brush or fill an enclosed area. You can also start with a blank page.

Memory pairs

Find eight matching pairs. One player or take turns.

Tic-tac-toe

Two players: take turns making a line of three.

Sliding puzzle

Tap a tile beside the empty space. Put 1–15 in order, with the empty space last.

Word hunt

Find the nature words. They run left to right, down, or diagonally. Tap the first and last letters of a word.

Play away from the screen

Story circle · any age · no equipment

One person begins a story with a sentence. Each person adds another sentence. Try a silly animal adventure, a mystery, or a journey to a new planet. Younger children can add a sound or a gesture.

I spy & twenty questions · ages 4+ · no equipment

Choose something everyone can see and give its first sound or color. Others guess what it is. For twenty questions, choose an animal, object or place; the group has up to twenty yes/no questions to identify it.

Scavenger hunt · ages 3+ · ordinary objects

Find something soft, something round, three different colors, something that makes a quiet sound, and something useful. Use only a safe, agreed area; pointing counts, and no one needs to collect living things.

Dots & boxes · ages 6+ · pencil and paper

Draw a rectangular grid of dots. Take turns joining two adjacent dots with one line. Completing a square earns your initial in the square and another turn. When every square is closed, the most squares wins. Use the dot paper from Paper studio.

Fold a paper fan · ages 4+ · paper

Fold a narrow strip along one edge, turn the paper over, and fold an equal strip back. Keep folding back and forth. Pinch one end together and open the folds at the other. Decorate the paper first if you like.

Drawing prompts · any age · paper or drawing tool

Invent an animal with three unusual features. Draw a home on the moon. Design a helpful machine. Draw a map of an imaginary island. Take turns adding one line to a shared picture.

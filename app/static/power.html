2. Choose planning assumptions

Defaults are examples, not measured local conditions or battery specifications. Peak sun hours are equivalent full-output hours, not daylight duration. The solar factor should account for panel, wiring, controller and charging losses. Temperature, aging, shading, season and cloudy spells affect results.

Planning estimate

These energy estimates do not size protective devices, wire, transfer equipment or inverter surge capacity. Simultaneous starting loads can exceed the running wattage total. Use equipment instructions and applicable electrical/fire requirements for an actual installation.

Micro-hydro estimate

Power ≈ 9.81 × flow (L/s) × net head (m) × efficiency. Daily energy assumes that flow continues for 24 hours. Water rights, environmental permissions, seasonal flow and engineering remain separate requirements.

# Content sources: the landing-page collections

This guide follows the same order and grouping as the landing page: 23 content cards and 301 registered content items. Archives, datasets and grouped packs count as items; their internal articles, books, map features and individual pages are not separate cards.

Local links open saved content or browser tools on the Ark. Publisher links identify the original sources and require upstream internet. Saved editions are snapshots; a blocked publisher check does not mean the installed copy is missing. Long inventories expand below. Outstanding gaps are listed separately from installed material.

Generated from `dataark.yaml` and the dashboard card composition by `tools/document_content_sources.py` whenever the web site is deployed. See [Manage Library](/library.html) for current installation and update state.

::: details Caribbean island groups and destinations

The configured rectangular extraction spans longitude -89 to -58 and latitude 9 to 28, to zoom 15. It includes the following major island groups and adjoining coasts; this is a coverage overview, not an enumeration of every small island or a guarantee of feature completeness.

- Greater Antilles: Cuba, Hispaniola (Haiti and the Dominican Republic), Jamaica and Puerto Rico.
- Virgin Islands: U.S. Virgin Islands and British Virgin Islands.
- Northern/eastern Lesser Antilles: Anguilla, Sint Maarten / Saint Martin, Saint Barthelemy, Antigua and Barbuda, Saint Kitts and Nevis, Montserrat and Guadeloupe.
- Southern Lesser Antilles: Dominica, Martinique, Saint Lucia, Barbados, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Grenada, Trinidad and Tobago.
- Southern Caribbean: Aruba, Bonaire and Curacao.
- Other island groups: Bahamas, Turks and Caicos, Cayman Islands.

Bermuda is outside this extraction. The map viewer offers quick locations for Sint Maarten, USVI/BVI, Puerto Rico and the Bahamas.

## Scope and maintenance

This guide documents registered collections and linked tools. The Community board is a separate local service, not a twenty-fourth content-source card. USA.gov is an online-only reference and is not presented as an installed collection. Unregistered files and every article/book inside a ZIM are outside this item-level inventory. The wider [content-gap register](../20-Administration/ContentGaps.md) records acquisition work; use Manage Library and the catalogue source logs for current status.
